A clear transfer flow

From copied link to completed file.

Every stage has its own place, so a probe, retry, or history write does not interrupt the rest of the workspace.

    Paste and inspect

    LinkGrabber checks direct URLs in the background and keeps filename, host, size, and availability visible.

    Queue without blocking

    Start, pause, stop, reorder, and retry while transfer and disk work continue away from the interface thread.

    Return with context

    Downloads, LinkGrabber, and non-secret settings keep append-only local Git timelines for undo, redo, and restore.

Built around real transfer work

A desktop utility with visible state.

Transfer engine

Direct HTTP(S), handled in the background.

Redirect-aware probing, resumable .part transfers, bounded retry, collision policies, and global or per-host limits.

Local history

Undo without erasing what happened.

Undo, redo, and restore append a new local event. The prior timeline remains intact and stays on the device.

Desktop shell

Stable controls, focused navigation.

Global transfer actions and search stay in the top bar, core screens stay in the responsive rail, and Add links opens as a non-blocking drawer.
